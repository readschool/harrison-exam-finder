About

A free index of official past papers from UK exam boards, so you can search them all in one place.

How it works

Every week, Harrison Exam Finder reads the exam boards' public past-paper pages and records the address of each question paper, mark scheme and examiner report. Every night it checks that those addresses still work. When you choose a paper, you download it straight from the exam board's own website.

This site never stores or copies the papers themselves. The exam boards publish them and own the copyright, and some papers include material the boards have licensed from others.

What's covered

GCSE, AS and A-level papers in every subject that AQA, OCR and Pearson Edexcel publish them for. New qualifications are picked up automatically, and more boards will be added over time.

Papers from the most recent exam series don't appear until the boards release them to the public, usually the spring after the exam. Before then, only schools can get them, so ask your teacher. Boards also take older papers down after a few years, and when they do, they disappear from this index too.

No adverts, no accounts, no tracking

The site has no adverts and no paywall, and you never need to sign up. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Its fonts are served from this site, not from a third party.

Not an official service

Harrison Exam Finder is an independent project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by AQA, OCR, Pearson Edexcel or any other exam board. Always check you are revising for the right board and specification; your teacher can confirm which one you sit.

Report a problem

If a link is broken, a paper is in the wrong place, or you represent an exam board and have a concern, please open an issue on GitHub.
